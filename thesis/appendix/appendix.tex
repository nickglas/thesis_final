\section{Stage~K (Cross-Node Alternative): kind Multi-Worker CNI Sensitivity Data}
\label{sec:appendix-rq14b}

This section holds the appendix-only data tables for the Stage~K (cross-node) kind
multi-worker CNI sensitivity stage. The methodological framing of this
stage is given in \cref{sec:methods-rq14b}; the analytical reading and
its scope-bounding role in the deployment narrative are in
\cref{sec:analysis-rq14b}. As established in \cref{sec:methods-rq14b},
Stage~K (cross-node) is reported as supporting sensitivity evidence only, not as a
primary thesis-facing number: kind's worker ``nodes'' are Docker
containers sharing one host kernel and communicating over kind's CNI
bridge, so Stage~K (cross-node) measures CNI-bridge cost on a single physical host
rather than real cross-host networking. The genuine multi-host
inter-node penalty is measured separately in \cref{sec:rq15b} on AKS.

Functional parity was verified independently of timing:
\texttt{max\_abs\_diff}~$=0.0$ at \texttt{atol}~$=1\times10^{-5}$ for
all five conditions in both the local segment validation and the live
gRPC chain validation (\texttt{num\_inputs}~$=5$), recorded in
\texttt{merged\_results/parity\_validation.json}. Infrastructure
controls (image
\texttt{thesis-inference:latest} with pull policy
\texttt{IfNotPresent}, namespace \texttt{rq14b}, per-pod 1~CPU /
512~MiB Guaranteed-QoS service profile, per-pod 1~CPU / 1~GiB client
profile, one thread per pool, CPU affinity to core~0) were applied
uniformly across the five conditions, as preserved in
\texttt{merged\_results/deployment\_metadata.json} and
\texttt{merged\_results/environment.json}. Host-level controls were
weaker than in Stage~K: writes to \texttt{/sys} failed because those
paths were read-only in the containerised environment, the detected
governor was \texttt{schedutil} (rather than \texttt{performance}),
turbo was enabled, and the \texttt{nice~-5} request failed for
permission reasons (\texttt{environment.json}). This degraded
host-control state is one reason \cref{sec:analysis-rq14b} interprets
the comparison against Stage~K at the per-condition delta level only and
treats Stage~K (cross-node) as a kind-CNI sensitivity envelope rather than a clean
topology-only swap from Stage~K.

\chapter{Analysis}%
\label{ch:analysis}

This chapter interprets the empirical findings reported in
\cref{ch:experiments} and answers the research questions defined in
\cref{ch:introduction}. \Cref{sec:analysis-split,sec:analysis-deployment,sec:analysis-security} provide per-RQ interpretation grouped by research
strand and close with a cross-RQ synthesis. \Cref{sec:analysis-validation}
synthesises the credibility evidence across the whole thesis at the cross-RQ
level. \Cref{sec:analysis-evaluation} situates the system against the named
comparison points at the same cross-RQ level.
\Cref{sec:analysis-significance} reflects on the generalisability of the method
to other neural architectures and on the broader significance of per-layer
service decomposition. \Cref{sec:analysis-threats}
discusses the scope and generalisability of the analytical conclusions; the
thesis-wide limitations and threats to validity are consolidated in
\cref{sec:conclusion-limitations}.
